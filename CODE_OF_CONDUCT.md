# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other place where the project is represented.

## In short

- Be respectful. Criticise ideas and text, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or publishing of anyone's private information.
- Keep examples and reports free of real private data.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, Frank Goortani, using the
contact details at <https://goortani.com>. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer will follow the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
